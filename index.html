QUICK GUIDE

How to play

A few rounds, a steady hand, and a little luck.

01◎

Choose your play style

Start a camera match and allow camera access, or choose “Play with buttons” to play without a camera.

02✋

Make your move on GO

For camera play, show rock, paper, or scissors when the countdown reaches GO. Hold the hand in view briefly.

03♛

First to 3 wins

Rounds start automatically. Draws do not score. The first player to 3 round wins takes the match.

▣
Privacy by design

Camera hand tracking happens locally in your browser. The app does not send or store video. Match history is saved in this browser only. Camera play requires HTTPS or localhost.
